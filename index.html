心得How the board works

  1. 一

    Verify

    Type your lore name, then enter the code that shows up on your screen in game. That proves the character is yours.

  2. 二

    Claim

    Anyone in a squad can claim a mission for it. A squad holds one mission at a time, and each mission only takes the number of squads it asks for.

  3. 三

    Report

    Get the mission information from your assigned Chuunin and head out. Pay is handed out back in Kaigakure.
